Koḍi Habba at Koṭeśvara

The great chariot festival of Śrī Koṭiliṅgeśvara at Koṭeśvara, near Kundapura, on the full moon of Vṛścika.

Koḍi Habba is the annual rathotsava of Śrī Koṭiliṅgeśvara. Its name keeps the town's oldest name: the inscriptions call Koṭeśvara Kuḍikuru, or Koḍiyūru, and the festival of Koḍiyūru is the Koḍi Habba. It is first mentioned in an inscription of 1373 CE. Six days of utsava lead to the great chariot festival on the seventh; such a festival is called a Bhuvanotsava, held for the welfare of all.

These photos follow the eve of the festival, on 23 November 2026, and the day of the chariot, on 24 November.

Drawn from Śrī Kṣetra Koṭeśvara by Dr. Shankaranarayana Udupa (English edition, 2026, translated by Vedic Space).
